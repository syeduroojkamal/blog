- (**1885**) (Fail)  
-   Founder:  **Allan Octavian Hume**  (A.O. Hume), also known as the "Father of Indian Ornithology" .
-   Immediate Cause for the formation of Indian National Congress was  **ilbert Bill**  (1883).
-   The bill allowed senior Indian magistrates to judge cases involving British subjects.
-   It faced huge backlash from the British and had to be withdrawn, exposing the true face of British rule to Indians.
---
Dadabhai Naoroji
-   Known as the "Grand Old Man of India", he was a central figure in early Indian nationalism
-   First Asian to become a member of the British Parliament
-   Presided over 3 Congress Sessions: 1886, 1893, and 1906.
-   First to use the term "Swaraj" .
-   Prepared the first estimate of India 's National Income in 1876 (known as the "Father of National Income of India").
-   Book: "Poverty and  **Un-British Rule**  in India" (explained the "Drain of Wealth Theory").
-   Newspapers: "Rast Goftar" (Weekly for Parsi Community) and "Voice of India".
-   Started the "Rahnumai Mazdayasnan Sabha" movement in 1851 with Naoroji Furdonji and S.S. Bengali.

Important Congress Sessions

| Year | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1885 (1) | "Headed by W.C. Banerjee in Bombay | where 72 Delegates attended it (only 2 muslims). Demand for Indianisation of civil services." |
| 1886 (2) | "Headed by Dadabhai Naoroji in Calcutta | where 434 Delegates attended it. Simultaneous exams for civil services both in India and Britain" |
| 1887 (3) | Headed by Badruddin Tyabji in Madras. He was the first Muslim President of Congress |
| 1888 (4) | Headed by George Yule in Allahabad. He was the first British President of Congress |
| 1896 | "Held in Calcutta. Headed by Rahimtullah M. Sayani. The National Song “Vande Mataram” | written by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee in Anandmath | was sung for the first time by Rabindranath Tagore." |
| 1901 | Held in Calcutta. Mahatma Gandhi appeared in this INC Session for the first time. |
| 1905 | Headed by Gopal Krishna Gokhale in Benaras. Swadeshi Movement was formally adopted here. |
| 1906 | "Headed by Dadabhai Naoroji in Calcutta. Four resolutions: Swaraj | Swadeshi | Boycott | and National Education were adopted." |
| 1907 | Headed by Rash Behari Ghosh in Surat. In this Session the Congress split into Extremists and Moderates. |
| 1911 | "Held in Calcutta. Our National Anthem | “Jana Gana Mana” written by Rabindranath Tagore was first sung in this Session." |
| 1916 | Headed by Ambika Charan Mazumdar in Lucknow leading to merger of Congress. A Joint Pact also known as Lucknow Pact was signed between the Muslim League and Indian National Congress. |
| 1917 | Headed by Annie Besant in Calcutta (first Woman President of Congress) |
| 1924 | Headed by Mahatma Gandhi (only Session presided by him) in Belgaum (Karnataka). |
| 1925 | Headed by Sarojini Naidu (first Indian woman President of Congress and first women Governor of India state (Uttar Pradesh) in Kanpur. |
| 1929 | "Headed by Jawaharlal Nehru in Lahore. The INC passed the resolution for complete independence or Purna Swaraj & Civil Disobedience Movement (January 26 | 1930 was officially declared to be celebrated as first Independence Day)." |
| 1931 | Headed by Sardar Patel in Karachi. Meaning of Purna Swaraj Resolution Fundamental Rights & National Economic Policy |
| 1937 | Headed by J.L Nehru in Faizpur (first Session to be held in a village) |
| 1938 | held at Haripura (Gujarat) and S C Bose was elected as President of INC. |
| 1939 | "held at Tripuri and S C Bose was re-elected by defeating Gandhi's candidate Pattabhi Sitaramayya | but later S C Bose resigned." |
| 1946 | helf at Meerut and Headed by J.B. Kriplani |
